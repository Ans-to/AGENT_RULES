# Director's audit — Adam cut

## Result and limits
Rendered with HyperFrames 0.8.216: 58.4 s, 1920 × 1080, 30 fps, H.264 and stereo AAC. Adam is the approved Kokoro voice, not Chatterbox. Procedural Three.js meshes provide actual perspective 3D; the 960 × 540 WebGL canvas is upscaled to the 1080p composition. No Blender or stock photography was used. The type and transition overlays are HTML/GSAP. The selected narration, synthesized bed and 42 sound effects are mixed into the master. Exact phrase timing is estimated within measured narration segments, not word-aligned.

- `audit/ffprobe.json` records its streams and metadata. `audit/render-contact.jpg` samples the rendered output every four seconds; these show the actual 3D stages and type, not a substitute for reviewing the entire film.

Full results: `audit/rendercheck-final.json`. Initial pass was −15.9 LUFS and failed the preset; audio was raised 2 dB and remuxed with unchanged video, then retested. These checks do not measure artistic quality or intelligibility.
- HyperFrames check: overall pass, zero errors; 41 authoring-structure warnings (dense tracks/nested elements) and four software-WebGL performance warnings; zero craft/layout/motion/contrast/audio warnings. See `audit/hyperframes-check.json`.
- Video Analysis base pipeline passed for the final remux; sampled eight frames and extracted audio. Evidence gates and contact sheet are included. OCR and ASR were unavailable and disabled; no independent transcript or word-onset check. Speech and mix were not independently listened to in this environment.

## Unresolved deviations from the original motion script
This is a simplified adaptation, **not shot-by-shot conformance**. The eight narrative stages, central coin, network, gold link break, architecture, documents, containers and competing coins are present, but the original macro-engraving opening, 3D extruded type, foreground/line-matched physical occlusion transitions, fully choreographed camera fly-throughs, trading exchanges, detailed sequential network growth and several per-shot gestures are not implemented. The inter-scene transitions are overlay sweeps instead of matching moving geometry. Original beat-level timing and word highlights are not verified or implemented; text appears in estimated phrase windows. Several sound directions in the original plan are represented by synthetic generic ticks, impacts and noise sweeps rather than literal recorded objects. The music uses a repeating chord progression and pulse, not independently arranged section-specific layers/ducking. The frame samples support stage visibility only, not every requested detail. A human should review the full video, listen for speech clarity and synchronization, and decide whether this simplified 3D design meets the creative brief.
