Fighters generated with Ara 3D Gen, https://github.com/olatunde85/3d-gen (MIT), using MakeHuman CC0 character data from https://github.com/makehumancommunity/makehuman and https://github.com/abbashaji/assets. The boxing motion is from the CMU Graphics Lab Motion Capture Database (subject 13, trial 17), created with funding from NSF EIA-0196217, converted by cgspeed and retargeted with Ara 3D Gen. Other clips included in the GLB are Ara procedural animation or CMU mocap as documented in the Ara project.
